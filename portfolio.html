Industry Award — Communication Engineering · EECS 2026

Smart Posture Correction (C2G05)

9th EE Capstone Showcase · Universiti Teknologi Malaysia · 14 January 2026 · Pulai Springs Resort, Johor

Sponsored by: Emcovit · DFS · Engie for Good · Flex · CanSec · IEEE

A hybrid wearable + computer vision system that detects bad sitting posture in real-time and delivers immediate haptic correction. The host PC runs a Python/OpenCV pipeline for posture classification; the result is sent via UDP to a custom ESP32 PCB worn on the body, which triggers vibration alerts. Monitoring and notifications are handled via the Blynk IoT platform.
